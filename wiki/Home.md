# Swiggy Instamart Price Tracker Wiki

Welcome to the comprehensive guide for the Swiggy Instamart Price Tracker! This tool helps you automatically track deep discounts and pricing errors on Swiggy Instamart.

## 🎯 What This Tool Does

Automatically monitors Swiggy Instamart for:
- Products priced under ₹50
- Discounts over 70%
- Pricing errors (₹9, ₹10 deals)
- Sends instant Telegram notifications
